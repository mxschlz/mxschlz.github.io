### Workshops & Seminars

Here is a summary of workshops, seminars, and tutoring activities I have conducted for students and researchers:

*   **EEG Workshop** (June 2026)  
    *Held at the **HörHanse Tage 2026** (Lübeck, Germany)*  
    Conducted an interactive workshop on Electroencephalography (EEG) application, experimental setup, and analysis pipelines in front of approximately 50 participants.
    
*   **Statistics & EEG Application Seminars** (Occasional)  
    *Held for students at the **University of Lübeck** and **University of Leipzig***  
    Instructed seminars covering statistical methods and practical application of EEG. Focus areas included experimental design, preprocessing, and electrophysiological data analysis.
    
*   **Psychoacoustic EEG Experimentation Tutoring** (2020 – 2024)  
    *Held at the **University of Leipzig** (Neurobiology Group)*  
    Tutored students on how to prepare, conduct, and analyze psychoacoustic EEG experiments using Python and PsychoPy.
